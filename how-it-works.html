Simple, Transparent Process

From booking to a spotless finish

Here's exactly what happens when you book with Onyx Auto Spa — no surprises, no hidden steps.

1

Book online in minutes

Pick your service, vehicle type, and a date & time that suits you on our booking page. No account required.

2

Get instant confirmation

You'll receive a confirmation email and a reminder text the day before your appointment, including your technician's estimated arrival window.

3

We arrive fully equipped

Our mobile unit carries its own water and power supply, so we can work at your home, office, or apartment car park — no hookups needed.

4

Pre-service walkaround

Your technician does a quick walkaround with you to note any problem areas — stains, scratches, pet hair — before starting.

5

Wash and/or detail

Using premium, paint-safe products, we work through your chosen package: wash, interior detail, paint correction, or all three.

6

Final quality check

Every job ends with a panel-by-panel inspection against our quality checklist before we call it done.

7

Enjoy your ride

Pay securely online or on the day, and step into a showroom-fresh vehicle. Not happy? We'll make it right — that's our guarantee.

Good To Know

What to expect on the day

Most jobs take 45–120 minutes

Depending on the package and vehicle size, we'll give you an estimated duration when you book.

We come to you

Driveway, office car park, or apartment building — as long as there's parking, we can work there.

Fully insured technicians

Every Onyx technician is trained, background-checked, and covered by public liability insurance.
