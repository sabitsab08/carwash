Our Story

Built by people who care about cars

Onyx Auto Spa started with a simple idea: premium detailing shouldn't require a trip to a shop or a full day off work.

Since 2018

From one van to a Melbourne-wide fleet

We started with a single van, a small kit of professional-grade products, and a promise: treat every car like our own. Word spread, bookings grew, and today Onyx Auto Spa runs a full fleet of self-contained mobile units across Melbourne.

What hasn't changed is the standard. Every technician is trained in-house, every product is chosen for results — not just cost — and every job ends with the same quality checklist we used on day one.
